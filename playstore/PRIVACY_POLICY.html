Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the SummifySocial mobile application (package: com.summifysocial.app) and related web services ("SummifySocial", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how information is handled when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

SummifySocial is designed to respect user privacy. We minimize data collection to what is strictly required to provide news aggregation and fact-checking services:

  • Followed Creators & Topic Preferences: Stored locally on your device or in your private session so that your newsfeed displays stories matching your chosen categories.
  • User-Submitted Queries: If you use the "Fact-Check Any Post" feature, the text you submit is sent securely to our fact-checking verification engine solely to retrieve relevant public sources and calculate accuracy ratings.
  • Device & Network Diagnostics: Standard non-personally identifiable diagnostic information (such as operating system version and crash logs) may be processed to ensure app stability.

2. Third-Party Content & Social Media Feeds

SummifySocial displays publicly available posts and syndicated content from social networks (including X, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, and RSS feeds). When you click external links to view original content, you are redirected to the respective third-party platform governed by its own privacy policies.

3. AI Summarization & Fact-Checking Engine

Our automated summarization algorithms extract key factual claims and query public journalistic wire services and scientific archives to corroborate claims. No private user correspondence or personal credentials are accessed or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You can clear your cached feed and reset your followed creators at any time directly through the app settings or by clearing the app data in your Android device settings.

5. Children's Privacy

SummifySocial does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe such data has been collected, please contact us for immediate removal.
