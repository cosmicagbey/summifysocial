## 4. Full Description (Max 4,000 Characters)
```text
Tired of jumping between X (Twitter), Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube just to piece together breaking news and trending stories? Overwhelmed by sensational clickbait, rumors, and unverified claims flooding your feeds?

Welcome to SummifySocial — the intelligent social news aggregator that monitors your favorite creators, journalists, and news channels, summarizes the content into concise key takeaways, and cross-references facts against verified global sources in real time.

KEY FEATURES:

📰 ALL YOUR NEWS CREATORS IN ONE FEED
Follow creators, journalists, and official news outlets across X, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Reddit, and RSS feeds. SummifySocial collects the latest updates and presents them in a clean, unified dashboard with full media, photos, and direct links to original posts.

🧠 AI-POWERED SUMMARIZATION & TAKEAWAYS
Instead of wading through endless comment threads or clickbait videos, SummifySocial extracts:
• 1-2 sentence core summaries of the essential facts.
• 3-4 structured bullet-point key takeaways.
• Quick-view original post preview for full transparency.

🔍 LIVE ONLINE FACT-CHECKING & ACCURACY SCORES
Every breaking claim is automatically evaluated against credible news wires and scientific publications (including Reuters, Associated Press, Bloomberg, Nature, ESPN, and more).
• Accuracy Rating (0% to 100%): Instantly know whether a story is Verified True, Developing, Contested, or Debunked.
• Source Citations: Direct clickable links to the independent articles and official statements backing up the audit.

🏷️ CATEGORIZATION & SMART FILTERING
Filter through the noise effortlessly:
• Tech & AI
• Science & Space Exploration
• Sports & Tournaments
• Entertainment & Pop Culture
• Business & Financial Markets
• World News & Politics

⚡ REAL-TIME LIVE UPDATES
Stay ahead of breaking developments as they happen with instant live updates and new story alerts.

🛡️ FACT-CHECK ANY POST ON DEMAND
Encountered a suspicious viral claim on social media? Paste the text or link into SummifySocial to generate an instant fact-check analysis with corroborating evidence.

Stop wasting time doomscrolling unverified feeds. Download SummifySocial today and experience social news with clarity, context, and verifiable facts.
```
